# Communication Policy

`AGENTS.md` (Final Response, `plan`, Owner Gates) defers to this file for
register. Owner instruction, 2026-07-25: **too much text.**

- Default to a few sentences. Bottom line first, then only what the owner must
  act on or could not have assumed.
- Cut: restating what was asked, design rationale already written to the plan,
  verification command lists that passed, section headers on short replies,
  and closing offers of further work.
- Keep: what broke, what the owner must decide, and anything unverified or
  still open. Never shorten by dropping a caveat — put the caveat in a clause.
- Detail belongs in `.agents/` files and finding records, where it is durable.
  The chat reply points at it; it does not reproduce it.
- Owner gates stay self-contained per `AGENTS.md` — brief, but still answerable
  cold without scrollback.
