## New design findings

1. **HIGH — The media build excludes codecs the owner requires to play locally.** A-2 describes its software codecs as examples, but A-3 explicitly limits the build to the decoders A-2 names. An FFV1 video in an MKV is a concrete casualty. FFmpeg has the decoder, yet the prescribed build leaves it out, so Original playback fails. Derive the build from the full local-playback requirement, include the permitted software decoders and demuxers, and dispatch to software whenever Apple cannot decode the actual stream. [ipados-port.md:334](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:334) · [ipados-port.md:453](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:453) · [decisions.md:2796](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/decisions.md:2796) · [FFmpeg codec support](https://www.ffmpeg.org/general.html)

2. **HIGH — The plan still routes media as though P2 chose one renderer.** P2 is told to play software-decoded video through both renderers, even though the loopback cannot accept decoded frames. A-2 also sends ordinary compressed video through whichever renderer P2 chose. If Dolby Vision needs loopback, following those instructions can reject a viable architecture or send unrelated titles through a Dolby-only path. Make sample buffers the route for ordinary playback and software decode. Limit P2’s choice to the Dolby Vision profiles and devices that need loopback. [ipados-port.md:162](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:162) · [ipados-port.md:176](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:176) · [ipados-port.md:185](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:185) · [ipados-port.md:334](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:334)

3. **HIGH — The loopback has no production encoder for lossless audio.** If Dolby Vision requires loopback and the selected track is TrueHD or DTS-HD MA, A-2 produces multichannel LPCM. The fallback requires ALAC or FLAC, while the build names decoders and muxers only. A muxer cannot turn LPCM into either encoded format. This breaks the UHD-remux combination the fallback is intended to serve. If P2 selects loopback, include a local lossless encoder and assign it responsibility for channel layout, timestamps, and the audio rendition served to AVPlayer. [ipados-port.md:180](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:180) · [ipados-port.md:361](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:361) · [ipados-port.md:453](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:453)

4. **HIGH — The loopback can lose Dolby Vision configuration during remuxing.** With the pinned FFmpeg 9 build’s ordinary MP4 muxer settings, the muxer can receive Dolby Vision side data and still omit the `dvcC` or `dvvC` box. A correct master playlist then advertises Dolby Vision while the initialization segment lacks its decoder configuration. AVPlayer can miss Dolby Vision, producing a false conclusion about the device. Carry the output profile’s configuration into the MP4 track and configure the muxer to emit the corresponding box and sample entry. [ipados-port.md:152](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:152) · [ipados-port.md:345](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:345) · [versions.txt:18](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/packaging/libmpv/versions.txt:18) · [FFmpeg’s MP4 writer](https://ffmpeg.org/doxygen/9.0/movenc_8c_source.html#l03000)

5. **HIGH — App Transport Security can block the new playback client.** The plan moves stream fetching to `URLSession`, but its plist allowances cover web-view artwork and local networking. A user-configured, fully qualified HTTP media-server address outside those exceptions can work in the Rust library client and fail when playback starts. ATS can also prevent a `URLSession` delegate from honoring the approved certificate-check override. Specify a playback transport that supports these configurations, such as the core’s HTTP client feeding custom I/O, while preserving the per-request origin policy. [ipados-port.md:308](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:308) · [ipados-port.md:551](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:551) · [decisions.md:2302](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/decisions.md:2302) · [Apple’s ATS rules](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/security/preventing-insecure-network-connections)

6. **HIGH — Automatic cookies can cross the engine’s origin boundary.** `URLSession` supplies stored cookies by default. If a media-server proxy sets a session cookie and a playlist redirects to another HTTPS port on the same host, the second service can receive that cookie even after the engine strips Vela’s provider headers. Cookies have no port isolation. Disable automatic cookies for media requests, or explicitly attach required cookies only after the same origin check used for provider credentials. [ipados-port.md:308](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:308) · [Apple’s cookie-setting default](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/urlsessionconfiguration/httpshouldsetcookies) · [RFC 6265 §8.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6265.html#section-8.5)

7. **MEDIUM — Redirected playlists can resolve relative segments against the wrong URL.** If `/play/master.m3u8` redirects to `/session/42/master.m3u8`, a relative child belongs under `/session/42/`. The custom-I/O contract passes bytes to FFmpeg. Without the final response URL, FFmpeg can resolve the child under `/play/` and fail playback. Pass the effective URL for every playlist, or resolve and clean its references against that URL before handing it to FFmpeg. [ipados-port.md:305](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:305) · [ipados-port.md:329](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:329) · [FFmpeg’s playlist-base handling](https://ffmpeg.org/doxygen/9.0/hls_8c_source.html#l00871) · [RFC 3986 §5.1.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3986.html#section-5.1.3)

8. **MEDIUM — The new playlist rule mislabels Dolby Vision profile 5.** The plan directs the writer to include a Dolby compatibility brand in `SUPPLEMENTAL-CODECS` for the loopback. Profile 5 has no HDR-compatible base to advertise that way. Apple’s profile 5 examples put `dvh1.05…` directly in `CODECS` with `VIDEO-RANGE=PQ`. Applying the blanket rule can misdeclare a required profile. Generate the attributes from the actual profile and compatibility ID. [ipados-port.md:145](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:145) · [ipados-port.md:156](/Users/michael/Dev/vela/.agents/plans/ipados-port.md:156) · [Apple’s profile-specific playlist examples](https://developer.apple.com/streaming/hls-authoring-specification-for-apple-devices.html)

9. **MEDIUM — The bounded segment store cannot by itself support full-film seeking.** When the viewer seeks back beyond retained segments, AVPlayer can request a segment the producer has discarded. Limiting the playlist to retained segments instead makes the beginning of the film unreachable. Keep the byte cap and give the local server a seekable segment index with on-demand remuxing from source keyframes. 10. **MEDIUM — The custom player cannot delegate display matching and Now Playing to bare `AVPlayer`.** The loopback description assigns both to AVPlayer itself. Vela presents a custom layer and controls. On Apple TV, relying on that statement can leave the display in SDR during the Dolby Vision probe. On iPad, it can leave system playback controls without metadata or handlers. Keep the explicit display-manager and Now Playing responsibilities on the loopback path. After a seek, pause, or buffering stall, subtitles can advance independently of the picture. Schedule overlays from the active renderer’s media time, using the current player item on the loopback path.
