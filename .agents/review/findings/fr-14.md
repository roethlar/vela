# fr-14: the end-to-end judge let a play report its stop twice

**Severity**: MEDIUM — The end-to-end judge let a play report its stop twice.
**Status**: Verified — codex wrote the repair; reviewed by the working agent, 2026-11-16
**Branch**: —
**Commit**: `9171b2e`

## Evidence

Reviewer, verbatim: "tests/e2e/judge.mjs:137–143 accepts two matching stopped reports without establishing a failed first delivery. Duplicating the unconditional stopped emission at tests/e2e-judge.test.mjs:755, where no transport failure or retry occurs, left all 25 pa-7 tests passing. The mutation was restored."

## Predicted observable failure

Reviewer, verbatim: "A regression sending the final report twice after successful delivery passes the same judge used by multiplex. This is a MEDIUM test false negative: duplicate finalization is classified as a legitimate retry."

Reviewer's suggested direction, verbatim: "Keep legitimate retries supported, but separately guard successful delivery against duplicate logical final reports. Exercise retries through a controlled connection failure and red-proof the success guard by duplicating the final tracker send."

## What

Admitted. `c618d31` had made the judge of `multiplex` (`plexStopProblems`)
accept one identical copy of Plex's stopped report, on the reasoning that
since `e50aa83` a report whose connection failed is sent again and both
copies can arrive. Nothing established that a first delivery had failed, so
a regression that sends the final report twice after a successful delivery
passed the judge. A fault of the tests, in the repair of `pa-7` that had
landed hours before.

The working agent did not take the reviewer's direction (keep the allowance
and guard successful delivery separately) but a simpler one, and told the
coder why: in this venue a legitimate retry does not arrive twice. The
retry is for a pooled connection the server closed while it was idle, and a
report written into that connection is never handled by the server, so the
mock records one arrival, the retry's. `multiplex` had passed in both
players with the retry in place and a judge that demanded exactly one (the
full runs at `013cee3`). An allowance no scenario needs is what lets a real
double report through. The coder was told to keep the allowance and say so
if it found a way the mock legitimately records two; it found none.

## Approach

What landed (`9171b2e`), in its commit's words:

c618d31 made the judge of `multiplex` accept one identical copy of
Plex's stopped report, reasoning that a report whose connection failed
is sent again (e50aa83) and both copies can arrive. Nothing established
that a first delivery had failed, so a play that reports its stop twice
after a successful delivery would have passed (review finding fr-14).

The allowance is gone and the judge demands exactly one stopped report,
the last, as it did before. In this venue a legitimate retry does not
arrive twice: the retry is for a pooled connection the server closed
while it was idle, and a report written into that connection is never
handled, so the mock records one arrival, the retry's. Two arrivals here
are worth a failed run and a look.

Written by codex, on the working agent's ruling of the direction (the
reviewer suggested keeping the allowance and guarding delivery
separately; codex was told to keep it and say so if the mock could
legitimately record two, and found no such path). Reviewed by the
working agent: the judge's tests pass, 75 of 75; `node
tests/pa7-redproof.mjs` catches 49 of 49 mutations, the reviewer's
duplicated final report among them; and `multiplex` passed three times
in each player with one stopped report each time.
